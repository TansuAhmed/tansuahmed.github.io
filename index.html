About

I study the coast of Bangladesh, a low-lying delta exposed to tidal flooding, erosion and climate change. My work uses two tools: satellite observation and numerical modelling.

Since January 2020 I have been a Research Assistant at the Coastal Ocean Observation and Prediction Laboratory, University of Chittagong. I process satellite and in-situ data in ArcGIS Pro, QGIS and ENVI to map turbidity, water quality and coastal habitats.

For my M.S. thesis, funded by an NST Research Fellowship, I set up the Delft3D-FM model of the Bangladesh coast of the Bay of Bengal and validated it against tide gauge data from Chittagong Port and the Sundarbans coastal area.

I am also a researcher at the Hydro-Climate and Ocean Centre (HCO), where I am lead applicant for an R&D project on CDOM and salinity along the Karnaphuli estuary, submitted to the Ministry of Science and Technology (under review).

Next, I want to bring satellite observation and a validated tidal model together to map coastal flooding for early warning.
